Cleaner lists: no more row of empty tick boxes.

- **On a computer**, an item's tick box appears when you point at the row (or
  reach it with the keyboard). Once you tick one, all of them show so you can
  keep going. "Select all" at the top is always there.
- **On a phone**, lists show no tick boxes at all. Tap the new **Select**
  button next to the search box, or press and hold an item, then tap items to
  pick them. Each item's picture turns into its tick. Tap × in the bar at the
  bottom when you're done.
- **The filter button on phones** shows how many filters are on as a small
  badge in its corner, instead of pushing the icon aside.
