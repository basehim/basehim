A new look for Settings.

- Settings now look like the dashboard: each group of settings is a card,
  with every setting's name and explanation on the left and its control on
  the right.
- On/off settings are switches instead of checkboxes.
- On phones, Settings works like a phone's own settings app: a list of
  sections, a back button, large titles, and a Save button always at the
  bottom.
- Moving between sections is instant: no page reload, on desktop or phone.

After updating: nothing to do.
