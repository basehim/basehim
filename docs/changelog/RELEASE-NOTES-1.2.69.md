Fixes 1.2.68, which stopped sites running the Analytics app from loading.

Everything in 1.2.68 is included:

- New: choose what's on your dashboard. Press **Customize** to drag cards into
  place, change their width, remove them, or add more from **Add widgets** —
  including widgets from your apps. Your layout follows you to every computer
  and phone you sign in from.
- New: cards have their own settings, a Refresh button, and can be collapsed.
- New: Publishing activity, Site health and Your recent activity cards.
- New: administrators can make their layout the default for everyone.
- Security: the dashboard no longer shows drafts and comments waiting for
  moderation to people who aren't allowed to see them.

After updating: nothing to do. If your site installed 1.2.68 and stopped
loading, install this over it (or upload `app/Core/App.php` from this
release) and it will load again.
