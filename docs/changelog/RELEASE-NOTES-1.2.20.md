Profile photo editor.

- Change your photo with the pencil on your avatar (My Profile, or Edit User
  for people you manage). A window opens over the blurred page: upload a photo,
  or pick one from the Media Library.
- Crop it in a round frame before saving — drag to position, zoom, rotate.
- The new photo appears straight away everywhere in the admin: the top bar,
  the Users list and the Edit User screen.
- The comment form shows your current photo beside "Commenting as".
- Fixed: the author box photo could appear tiny with some themes.
- Includes everything in 1.2.19, so it can be installed on 1.2.18 or 1.2.19.
