- **At a glance has real charts:** each card shows a small chart of its own
  figures instead of the decorative bars it had:
  - **Posts:** a trend line of posts published each week;
  - **Pages:** published against drafts;
  - **Media:** the library's size by kind of file (images, documents, video);
  - **Comments:** comments received each week;
  - **People:** who has signed in during the last 30 days.

  Hover a bar or point to see its week and count.
- **Calmer colours:** the card icons are soft blue squares instead of solid
  blue, so the charts and what needs you stand out.
- **Clearer figures:**
  - the card says what it covers ("Charts: last 12 weeks · changes: last
    30 days");
  - a small site shows "+13 new" instead of a percentage like "+260%";
  - Comments shows its trend, with the review count below it in blue;
  - Pages says when a page was last updated;
  - "4,622 published" and other hints have thousands separators.
- **On a phone:** every card's number sits at the same height. A new or
  quiet site shows "No posts published in the last 12 weeks" and similar,
  instead of an empty chart.
- **AI assistants (MCP):** posts and pages can be scheduled or backdated,
  and scheduled posts can be listed.
- **Error pages:** "Access denied", "Page not found" and the error pages
  fit a phone screen and use the admin's own buttons.

After updating: nothing to do. No database changes. Apps' dashboard cards
look as before; they can add the same charts (see Dashboard widgets in the
docs).
