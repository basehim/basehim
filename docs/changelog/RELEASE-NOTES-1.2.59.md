One marketplace for apps and themes, with a smoother install flow.

- New: Apps and Themes now share a single **Marketplace** page, linked from the
  sidebar. Old marketplace links still work — they redirect to it.
- New: after you install an app, the button becomes **Activate** right there. It
  then asks you to allow the permissions the app needs to work, showing each
  one's risk so you can leave any out before turning the app on.
- Improved: the Apps page has been redesigned to match the rest of the admin —
  clearer status, permissions and actions.

After updating: nothing to do. Your installed apps and their permissions are
unchanged.
