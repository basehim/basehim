One broken app can no longer take your site down.

- If an app cannot load or start — after an update, or because it clashes
  with Basehim's own code — Basehim switches that app off and keeps
  everything else running: your pages, the admin and your other apps.
- The Apps screen shows the app in red with the reason, and every admin page
  tells you until it is dealt with. **View report** explains what went wrong,
  when, and where.
- Once the app is fixed (a corrected version, or edited files), **Check and
  reactivate** turns it back on — only if it now passes Basehim's checks.
- Basehim now checks an app's code before it runs: when it is installed,
  updated or activated, and whenever its files change.
- Nothing is deleted: the app's files, settings and data are kept.

After updating: nothing to do. The first page after the update takes a moment
longer while Basehim checks your active apps. If one of them would have broken
the site, it is switched off and the Apps screen says why.
