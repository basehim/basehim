The admin works better on phones, tablets and with a keyboard, and more screens look and behave the same way.

- **Menu on phones and tablets:** Escape closes it, and keyboard users no longer have to tab through the hidden menu to reach the page.
- **Posts, Pages, Comments and other lists on phones:** each item's date, views and author now sit on their own line under a full-width title, instead of being squeezed into a narrow column.
- **Users** is now a list like Posts and Pages: search, filter by role or status, sort, choose columns, and readable cards on phones. The role filter now works (it used to offer only "Roles").
- **API Keys:** a cleaner New key window that opens as a sheet on phones, and a key list that reads well on small screens.
- **Categories, Tags, Menus and Widgets** use the same buttons, fields, tabs and checkboxes as the rest of the admin, and their lists fit small screens without sideways scrolling.
- **Easier to tap:** larger checkboxes and copy buttons, and a bigger "Settings" back link on touch screens.
