Recent Posts thumbnails now fill their space properly, and you can choose their shape.

Small square thumbnails in the Recent Posts widget could show a gap instead of
filling their square in some themes. They now always fill it.

A new Thumbnail shape setting lets you pick square or wide 16:9 thumbnails
(cropped to fill), or keep each image's original shape so nothing is cut off.
Existing widgets keep their square thumbnails until you change the setting.
