A better way to manage the widgets in your theme's sidebars and footers.

- Appearance → Widgets → Widget areas is rebuilt: drag widgets to reorder them or move them between areas (or use the arrow keys), add them from a searchable list, and change settings without reloading the page.
- Each widget can be hidden without deleting it, duplicated, given its own CSS class, shown without its title, and previewed before you save.
- Settings are checked when you save, with a clear message beside anything that needs fixing, instead of being saved as typed.
- Widgets from a previous theme or a switched-off app are kept and listed, not lost, and come back when the theme or app does.
- New options: Recent Posts by tag or author and excerpt length; Categories with sub-categories under their parent; Categories and Tags as a cloud, and leaving some out; a button label for Search.
- Widget lists load faster for visitors: the output is cached and refreshed whenever your content changes.
- Dates in Recent Posts now follow your site's timezone.

After updating: nothing to do. Your widgets and their settings stay as they are.
