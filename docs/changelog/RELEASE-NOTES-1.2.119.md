A tidier dashboard and a refreshed menu.

- **Dashboard welcome card.** On phones, New post and Upload now sit side by
  side at equal width, and Customize is a small icon in the card's corner. On
  computers, Customize is a small icon at the end of the buttons.
- **A cleaner side menu.** The page you're on has one clear highlight, every
  item is the same height, and sub-pages hang off a thin guide line.
- **Menu search finds the exact page.** Searching "tags" now shows just Tags,
  not every page under Posts. Press **/** to jump to the search box.
- **More room for the menu.** The bottom of the menu is one slim row with the
  version and update status, and "View site" is now the address under your
  site's name. Clicking the logo takes you to the Dashboard.
- **Collapsed menu keeps its alerts.** Waiting comments now show as a small
  dot instead of disappearing.
