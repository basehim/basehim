A new admin bar on your site, for everyone who manages it.

- While signed in, you now see a bar on your site's pages. From it you can edit the page you're looking at (or make quick changes to its title, address and status without leaving the page), create new content, search everything you can edit (Ctrl+K), and see what needs attention: updates, comments waiting for approval, failing tasks and security warnings.
- The bar knows the page you're on. On a post you get its details and edit links, on a category its edit link, on search results the same search in the admin, and on a missing page why it's missing.
- Choose a slim bar across the top, or a floating button that opens a panel. On phones the bar shows icons and opens full-width menus.
- Settings → Admin bar: turn it off, choose who sees it, the colours, size, sections and what users may change. Each person can also pick their own style from the bar, or turn it off in their profile.
- Visitors never see the bar, and their pages load nothing extra.
- Apps can add their own menus, live information and alerts to the bar.
- Your pages no longer give every visitor a session cookie, which makes them easier to cache.
- The Basehim logo in the editor's corner is now white, so it's easy to see.

After updating: nothing to do. The bar is on for every role except Subscriber. To change that, go to Settings → Admin bar.
