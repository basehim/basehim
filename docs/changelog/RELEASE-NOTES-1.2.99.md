Tidier lists, more control over your logo, and more reliable email.

- **Posts, Pages and other lists:** in the Comfortable layout (the default), each row's actions now appear as simple links under the row when you point at it: View or Preview, Edit, Quick Edit, Publish or Move to Draft, and Move to Trash. Nothing on the page moves when they appear. In the Compact layout (Screen Options → Density), the ⋮ menu is back at the end of each row, and it is now larger and easier to see.
- **Customize → Site identity:**
  - Your logo and site icon now show in full instead of cropped.
  - A new **Logo width** slider resizes the logo on your site as you drag it.
  - New **Site name** and **Site tagline** switches hide either one from the header, for example when your logo already shows your name.
  - "Site description" is now called **Site tagline**, the same as in Settings.
- **Home page title:** browser tabs and search results now show your site name and tagline, such as "Northwind Studio – Design and print", using the separator you chose in Settings → SEO. A home page title you set in Settings → SEO still comes first.
- **Email:** long messages are no longer broken by mail servers, every message carries a Message-ID that spam filters look for, and sender names with commas or quotes now arrive correctly.

After updating: nothing to do. To resize your logo or hide the site name or tagline in the header, open Customize → Site identity.
