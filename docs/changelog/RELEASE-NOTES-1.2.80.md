A calmer, friendlier dashboard.

- One colour: cards and buttons now use a single blue and its shades. Red
  only appears for errors and things that delete.
- Every card and button matches: the same corners, spacing and sizes across
  the dashboard and the rest of the admin.
- Nothing is cut off: card titles, descriptions, tile names and details wrap
  onto a second line instead of ending in "…".
- A warmer welcome: the dashboard greets you with good morning, afternoon or
  evening in your own timezone, today's date and your picture, with New post
  as the main button.

After updating: nothing to do.
