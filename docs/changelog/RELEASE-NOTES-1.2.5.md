Preview now works for every draft post.

Clicking Preview on a draft could show a "not found" page. It happened when
your permalinks are set to include the category and the post had a category,
and for every draft when permalinks are set to the post name alone. Preview now
opens the draft whatever your permalink setting and whether or not a category
is assigned.

If Preview failed for you before this update, your browser may still remember
the broken address. It now works too, so there is nothing to clear.

Also fixed: a visitor who guessed the address of an unpublished post could tell
that it existed. Unpublished posts now look exactly like pages that do not
exist to anyone who is not allowed to preview them. And previewing a draft no
longer adds to its view count.
