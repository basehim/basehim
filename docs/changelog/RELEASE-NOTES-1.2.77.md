A fresh look for the dashboard.

- Every card has a coloured icon, its title and a short description, with
  Refresh and a ⋮ menu beside them. Collapse is now in the ⋮ menu.
- At a glance shows each count in its own coloured tile, with how much it
  grew in the last 30 days, the comments waiting for review, and the people
  who signed in most recently.
- Publishing activity has its period right in the card header, with a new
  "This year" option, and a clearer chart.
- Numbers count up and charts grow in when they load. Nothing moves if your
  device is set to reduce motion.
- New post, Upload and Customize are larger buttons that are easier to tap.
- Buttons, cards and fields across the admin share the same rounded, softer
  style.

whatever was chosen.
