Long lists are easier to work with.

- **The search, filters and the table's column headings now stay in view** as
  you scroll down a long list, and the controls shrink a little to take less
  room once they stick.
- **The pager stays at the bottom of the screen** and is more compact, so you
  can jump pages without scrolling all the way down.
- **Changing page takes you back to the top** of the list, at the first row.
- You can turn either of these off under **Screen Options → Sticky** — they are
  on by default, and the choice is remembered in your browser.

Desktop and phones both benefit. After updating: nothing to do.
