Smaller fixes: a simpler installer, a smoother Media Library and dashboard.

- The installer no longer asks for a database table prefix. Each new site gets
  its own random one, so several sites can share a database safely.
- Media Library: images show a calm placeholder of the right shape while they
  load, and a file that cannot be previewed says so. The file details window
  now works like Quick Edit — header, Save and Close at the bottom, and full
  screen on phones.
- Dashboard: cards that load their own figures (such as Analytics) no longer
  stay on "Loading…" after you refresh or expand them.
- Dashboard: on phones, New post, Upload and Customize sit on one line.
- Saving your preferences in two tabs at once no longer loses one of them.
- The installer shows the right version number.

After updating: nothing to do. Existing sites keep their table prefix.
