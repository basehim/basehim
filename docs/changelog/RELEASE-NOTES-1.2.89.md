Author pages now pass Google's structured data test.

- Author pages tell search engines who the author is: name, bio, photo and
  number of posts. Google's Rich Results test no longer reports
  "Missing field mainEntity".
- Articles point to the same author, so search engines connect a writer's
  posts to their profile.

After updating: nothing to do. Google picks up the change the next time it
visits your author pages; you can check one in the Rich Results test.
