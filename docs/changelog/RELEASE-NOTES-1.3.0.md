- **Light, dark or your device's setting:** choose it in your account menu
  (your picture, top right). Each person chooses for themselves, and the
  whole admin follows: the editor, the Customizer, media, tables and the
  admin bar on your site.
- **Seven accent colours:** Default, Sunset, Rose, Amphibian, Strawberry, Kiwi
  and Pink LA, each a palette of four colours used for buttons, links and
  charts.
- **Status colours stay put:** success is always green and warnings and
  errors always orange and red, whatever accent you pick. A rising figure on
  the dashboard is green, a falling one red.
- **A colour palette for everyone:** ten named colours (red, orange, yellow,
  green, blue, pink, purple, brown, black, white) with ready-made classes,
  readable in light and dark, for the admin, apps and themes.
- **Tabs scroll with a mouse:** when tabs don't fit, arrows appear at the
  ends. The tab bar is frosted glass, like a table's sticky header. A table
  under tabs no longer slides behind them when you scroll.
- **Error pages match your look:** error and "access denied" pages follow
  your light or dark mode and accent colour.
- **Roomier dashboard on phones:** even spacing around cards, and the At a
  glance figures get more room.
- **No more flicker while pages load:** tables, the editor, the Customizer,
  media and settings pages appear finished, without a jump.
- **Easier to read:** small grey text, badges and buttons that were too
  faint now meet the accessibility standard for contrast, in light and dark.
- **Security:** the error log file many hosts create in your site's folder
  could be downloaded by anyone. It is now blocked; your site's settings
  file is updated for this the first time you open the admin after updating.
- **Apps can follow your choice:** app developers get colours and a script
  API that change with light, dark and the accent.

After updating: nothing to do. No database changes. Until someone chooses,
the admin follows their device's light or dark setting, with the Default
colour. If you use Analytics, update
it to 1.13.0 after this release.
