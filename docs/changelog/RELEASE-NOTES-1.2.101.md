Your sites stay in step with your update server, and lists get Quick View.

- **Update server:** after you install an update, your update server shows the new version straight away, not up to an hour later.
- **Your site's name, tagline and icon** are what your update server shows for your site, and a change in Settings or Customize reaches it right after you save.
- **Lists with several filters** show all of them at the top of the list, and a filtered list keeps its filters when you reload it or share its link.
- **Quick View:** lists that support it have a Quick View link that shows an item's details without leaving the list.
- **Apps:** updating an active app now always finishes its own setup steps correctly.
- **Large downloads** use far less memory on the server.

After updating: nothing to do.
