Make your site easy for AI assistants to find, read and use — and decide which AI crawlers may visit.

A new Settings → AI Agents page controls everything:

- Choose separately whether AI training crawlers, AI search crawlers, and AI
  assistants browsing for a person may visit your site. Your site now serves a
  proper robots.txt (it was missing before).
- Your site now publishes llms.txt — a summary of your site written for AI
  assistants — and a catalog that tells AI agents what your site offers.
- Forms on your pages, including the comment form, are described for AI
  assistants working in a visitor's browser, so they can fill them in
  reliably. The visitor still reviews and sends every form.

You can test the result in Google PageSpeed Insights, under Agentic Browsing.
