Faster pages with properly sized images, and a much more flexible Recent Posts widget.

Themes can now show each image at the right size — a small version in lists
and cards instead of the full photo — so pages load faster, especially on
phones.

If your images were imported (for example from WordPress), they may not have
these smaller versions yet. Go to Settings → Media and press Regenerate: it
works through your library with a progress bar, and can be stopped and
resumed.

The Recent Posts widget can now show thumbnails (small or medium), dates and
short excerpts, list your most viewed or most commented posts instead of the
latest, show posts from one category only, and leave out the post the reader
is already on. Existing widgets look the same until you change their settings.
