Content types from apps now work everywhere, apps can add custom fields, and the editor is easier to use on a phone.

- Apps that add their own kinds of content (events, products …) now get proper public pages, for example /events/summer-meetup, with a list page at /events. They appear in your sitemap and site search, and their admin list shows the right items. Before this update that list showed your posts instead, and links to the items didn't work.
- Apps can add extra fields to posts, pages and their own content (a date, a venue, a price …). The fields appear in the editor's sidebar and are checked when you save; anything invalid is kept as it was and the editor tells you which field to fix.
- Apps can add their own categories-style groupings, managed under their content's menu.
- In the editor, a line under the title shows the post's address, with Edit to change the slug and Preview / View. Preview also works on a new post: it saves a draft first. On a phone, Preview is in the ⋮ menu too.
- Editor panels follow what each kind of content uses, so an app's content only shows the settings it needs.

After updating: nothing to do. If you have a page whose address is the same as an app's content list (for example a page called "events" while an Events app is active), that page moves to /page/events.
