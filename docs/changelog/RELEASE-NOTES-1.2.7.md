Pages now live at the root of your site, every post has a category, and no two
posts or pages can share an address.

Pages: your pages are at yoursite.com/about-us rather than
yoursite.com/page/about-us. Old /page/ links, including the ones in your menus,
now redirect to the new address. On some sites those old links were showing an
error page; they now work.

Categories: a post saved without a category is filed under "Uncategorized",
so it always has a category address such as yoursite.com/uncategorized/my-post.
Choose a different default under Settings → Writing → Default Post Category.
Posts that had no category are filed under Uncategorized by this update; their
old addresses redirect to the new ones.

Addresses: a post can no longer take the same address as a page, or the
reverse. If an address is taken, the new item gets a number: about-us,
about-us-2, about-us-3. An address stays reserved while its item is in the
Trash, and becomes free again once the item is permanently deleted. Addresses
that the site itself uses, such as "search" or "feed", are skipped
automatically.

Also fixed: category and tag post counts crept upwards every time the Trash
was emptied. They are recounted by this update and stay correct from now on.
