Every author now has a page listing their posts, and posts can show an
"About the author" box.

Author pages are at yoursite.com/author/their-name, based on the author's
display name — never their login name. Each author can change their address
under Profile → Author page address. Only people who have published a post
have a page. Author pages are added to your sitemap.

The author box shows under each post with the author's picture, name, bio and
a link to their other posts. Add a bio and picture on the Profile page to fill
it in.

Both can be switched off under Settings → Reading.

Important: if your site had links to author pages using a login name (for
example /author/admin), those addresses no longer work. This is deliberate, so
that login names are never shown publicly.

Also fixed: the "Discourage search engines" option could not be switched off
once it had been turned on. It now can.
