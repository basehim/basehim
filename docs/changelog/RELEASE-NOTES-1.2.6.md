Comment counts now show everywhere, and signed-in members are no longer asked
for their name and email.

New comments waiting for approval now show as a number next to Comments in the
admin sidebar, and it updates by itself while the admin is open.

If your permalinks include the category or use the post name alone, posts were
not showing their comments or comment count at all. They now do.

Signed-in members commenting on your site are no longer asked who they are:
their comment is recorded under their account automatically. With the built-in
themes, the name and email fields are hidden for them. If your site uses a
custom theme, its comment form may still show those fields until the theme is
updated, but they are ignored for signed-in members.

Also fixed: the Reply button on comments was missing from the default theme.
