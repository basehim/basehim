Quick Edit for posts and pages.

- New: Quick Edit in the ⋮ menu of every row on the Posts and Pages lists.
  Change the title, slug, author, status, categories, tags or comments (posts),
  or the parent and order (pages), right in the list. Saving updates that row
  without reloading, and your search, filters, sorting and page stay as they
  were.
- Scheduling works here too: choose Scheduled to pick a date and time in your
  site's timezone, change the time of a scheduled post, or publish it now.
- Editing the title or anything else never changes a post's status or date: a
  draft stays a draft, a live post keeps its publication date, and a scheduled
  post keeps its time.
- Moving a live post back to draft (or anything that takes it off the site)
  asks you to confirm first.
- Each role sees only what it may change. Contributors can submit for review
  but cannot publish or schedule; only editors and administrators can change
  the author.
- A slug that is already used, reserved, or invalid is pointed out on the
  field instead of being changed silently. If you change the address of a
  published post, Quick Edit reminds you that old links will stop working.
- If someone else changed the same post while you were editing, Quick Edit
  tells you instead of overwriting their change. Nothing you typed is lost when
  a save fails.
