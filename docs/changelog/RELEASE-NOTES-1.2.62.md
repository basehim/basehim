Quick Edit, improved: the same fields as the editor, View and Preview, and a
more compact form.

- Quick Edit now uses the editor's own Categories and Tags boxes: tick
  categories from a list you can filter, and add tags as chips with
  suggestions as you type, plus your most used and recent tags.
- Quick Edit has the editor's Publish controls too: publish immediately or
  schedule for a date and time in your site's timezone, with a preview of
  exactly when it goes live. The Save button says what will happen —
  Publish, Schedule or Publish now.
- New: you can change the publication date of a published post, in the
  editor and in Quick Edit. Choose Change date: an earlier date keeps the post
  live with the new date; a later date schedules it (you are asked first,
  because it leaves the site until then).
- New: View and Preview in the ⋮ menu of each post and page. Published items
  show View; drafts, pending, scheduled and private items show Preview. Both
  open on your site in a new tab.
- The Quick Edit form is more compact: on wide screens the fields sit in a few
  short rows, and on phones they stack in one column.
- Opening the editor is lighter: it no longer loads every tag on the site.
