A dashboard you can arrange, and a security fix.

- New: choose what's on your dashboard. Press **Customize** to drag cards into
  place, change their width, remove them, or add more from **Add widgets** —
  including widgets from your apps. Save, and your dashboard looks the same on
  every computer and phone you sign in from.
- New: cards have their own settings (for example, how many posts to show), a
  Refresh button, and can be collapsed.
- New: Publishing activity, Site health and Your recent activity cards.
- New: administrators can make their layout the default for everyone who
  hasn't arranged their own. Anyone can go back with **Reset to default**.
- Security: the dashboard showed every signed-in person — subscribers and
  contributors too — the titles of other people's drafts and the text of
  comments waiting for moderation. Each card now shows only what that person
  is allowed to see.
- A card that can't load shows a message and a **Try again** button; the rest of
  the dashboard carries on.

After updating: nothing to do. Everyone starts with the default dashboard.
App developers: see docs/DASHBOARD-WIDGETS.md. Existing dashboard widgets keep
working.
