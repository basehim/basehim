Faster, smarter search everywhere: the search page, every admin list and the media library.

- Searches find every word in any order: "digitalocean pricing" finds "DigitalOcean Droplet Pricing", and "snipaste 2026" finds "Snipaste_2026-10-27.png". Use "quotes" for an exact phrase and -word to leave a word out.
- Search stays fast on large sites: a new search index answers in milliseconds even with a million posts, where searching used to read every one of them.
- The search page now shows the best matches first, includes your pages, and highlights the matching words. Choose what it covers under Settings → Search.
- Media, comments and users search the file name, caption, author and email as well.
- Admin lists remember your search when you reload or come back from editing.
- Apps can now add their own items to search.

After updating: open Settings → Search and click Build now, or leave it: the index builds in the background within a few minutes (longer on very large sites). Searches work as before until it is ready.
