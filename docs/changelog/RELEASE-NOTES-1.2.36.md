App updates now appear on the Updates page.

- Updates lists new versions of your installed apps next to Basehim's own,
  with release notes, and the sidebar badge counts them.
- Update apps one at a time, or press Update all: Basehim first, then each
  app. Downloads are checksum-verified, and an app keeps its settings and data.
- Apps can now describe themselves in app.json with an icon (PNG or GIF,
  256 or 512 px square, up to 1 MB), a description, a developer and a
  company, each with a link. Existing apps keep working unchanged.
