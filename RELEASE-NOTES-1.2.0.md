Apps can now add things to the front end of your site without anyone editing a
theme file.

Until now an app that needed to put something on the public site — an analytics
tag, a consent banner, a chat widget — had no way to do it. There was no place
for it to go, so apps had to give you a snippet and instructions for pasting it
into your theme.

Themes now include two lines that receive everything core and any installed app
needs. Install an analytics app and its script appears; remove the app and it
goes. Nothing to paste, nothing left behind.

Both bundled themes and the Circuits DIY theme have been updated. A theme that
has not been updated keeps working — it simply will not show anything an app
contributes, and its author can add the two lines whenever they like.

Also added: a set of small functions that make theme building less repetitive —
page-state classes for styling, and direct access to theme options and site
settings without the boilerplate every theme was writing for itself.
