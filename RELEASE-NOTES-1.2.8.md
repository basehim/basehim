The post editor now saves exactly what you edit.

Editing a published post or page in the visual editor could turn it back into
a Draft. It could also remove its categories and featured image, and reopen
comments. This happened whenever you clicked into the text to edit it before
saving. Your status, categories, featured image and comment setting now stay
exactly as they are unless you change them yourself.

Changes made in HTML (source) mode are now saved. Before, your edits were
replaced with the old content when you saved. Switching from the visual
editor to HTML now shows the post as proper HTML you can edit.

Media picker: images no longer overlap each other on phones or in larger
libraries, and the selected image is shown at a readable size on phones.

After updating, reload any post or page you had open in the editor.
