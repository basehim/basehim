Safer sign-in.

- The sign-in page now shows your site — its logo or icon, name, description
  and address, in your site's colour — instead of a generic Basehim screen.
- Two-step verification: after the password, a 6-digit code is emailed. Turn
  it on in My Profile, or require it for administrators or everyone in
  Settings › Authentication. "Don't ask on this device" skips it for up to
  90 days.
- My Profile › "Sign out everywhere else" ends every other session and
  connected app.
- Sign-in now pauses after repeated wrong passwords, and a password change
  signs out other devices. This release also fixes a sign-in redirect loop,
  password-reset link poisoning, and several ways round the sign-in
  protections (details in the changelog).
- Test your email (Settings › Email › Send test) before requiring codes.
